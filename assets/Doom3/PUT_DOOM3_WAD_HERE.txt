Place any supported standalone IWADs in this folder using these exact names:

Doom3.WAD
Tnt.wad
Plutonia.wad

The TV home screen always contains DOOM 3. It also adds separate
DOOM 3: TNT and DOOM 3: PLUTONIA entries when those named files are present.

The IWAD is not included in the redistributable mod package.

The known PrBoom-targeted download is repaired automatically into a separate
Runtime\Doom3.compat.WAD file. Your original Doom3.WAD remains unchanged.
Tnt.wad and Plutonia.wad are validated and launched without modification.
